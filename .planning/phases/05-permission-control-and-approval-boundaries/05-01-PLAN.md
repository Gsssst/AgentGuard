<objective>
Define the typed capability-tag and permission-decision contracts without invoking Tools. Preserve existing behavior when no policy is configured, while making configured authorization fail closed and distinguish direct denial from approval-required actions.
</objective>

<threat_model>
### Threats
- Unknown or malformed capability labels could accidentally bypass authorization.
- A partially authorized multi-label Tool could be treated as allowed due to unordered set iteration.

### Mitigations
- Validate labels at Tool registration and reject unknown/blank values.
- Implement explicit all-allowed / approval-trigger / deny precedence and test mixed-label cases.

### Residual risk
- Capability metadata is declarative and does not prove a Tool's real-world side effects; role identity and external authorization are deferred.
</threat_model>
