<threat_model>
### Threats
- Approval could be reused for modified arguments or another Tool.
- A malformed checkpoint could trigger a Tool before validation.
- A Router could replace the approved pending Action on resume.

### Mitigations
- Hash canonical unredacted Tool/action data and require an exact digest match.
- Validate the entire checkpoint and ApprovalDecision before emitting side effects.
- Consume the pending Action before requesting another Router decision.

### Residual risk
- SHA-256 is a binding digest, not an authenticated authorization token; real identity and signed approvals are deferred.
</threat_model>
