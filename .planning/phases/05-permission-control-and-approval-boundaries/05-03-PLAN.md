<threat_model>
### Threats
- Audit logs could leak secrets through Tool arguments or ACTION_PROPOSED events.
- Reports could claim approval success from incomplete event streams.

### Mitigations
- Centralize recursive redaction and apply it to every action/approval audit projection.
- Derive metrics from explicit events and terminal evidence; mark evidence inconsistent when required facts are absent.

### Residual risk
- Actor values are caller-provided labels until a future authenticated identity layer is implemented.
</threat_model>
